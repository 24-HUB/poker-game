# Provisional art

## Celestial collection

The twelve SVGs in `cosmetics/` are original repository-authored geometric
portraits and card-back patterns, generated from the editable source in
`scripts/generate-celestial-art.mjs`. They use no third-party characters,
photos, fonts or external artwork. The manifest records IDs, slots, rarities
and safe same-origin URLs. Reproduce with `node scripts/generate-celestial-art.mjs`.
The six portraits and matching backs are the approved M4 launch catalogue;
the built-in avatar/card back remain free defaults outside its pull pool.

## Historical prototype

`hostess.png` is the approved design-prototype artwork copied from
`prototype/assets/hostess.png`. It remains provisional and must not be presented
as final licensed production art. Replace it before a public release if its
provenance or usage rights cannot be confirmed.
